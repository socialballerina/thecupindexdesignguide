Making one yourself

Designing by hand, in Canva or Figma. Open the visual guidelines and the logo sheet, take the logo files from assets/, and build three 1080 × 1350 slides. Canva substitutes for the type are Poppins for Cup and Archivo for Index.

Designing in code. Copy the templates/ folder, drop your two photos over the placeholders keeping the filenames, then replace the venue name, the index number and the six scores. Preview it over a local server, python3 -m http.server 8000, because browsers block local file reads and these pages load their own components.

Handing it to an AI assistant. SKILL.md is a skill file. Clone the repo into your assistant's skills folder, or paste the file in as a brief. It stands alone.
