The grading process
01
Always unannounced, always paid for
Scored independently when we can go separately; if we sit at the same table, the two cards are averaged, never discussed first.
02
The same order, everywhere
One latte. Avocado toast if it exists. Banana bread for the believers. Nothing the staff recommends.
03
Six axes, scored at the table
Filled in before we leave, before the caffeine wears off, and before anyone compares notes.
04
Then we argue about the decimal
The composite is a mean, not a mood. The only thing genuinely up for debate is whether the banana bread counted as food.

Why an index
Every café has good light and five stars.
So why do you keep going back to exactly one of them?
Five stars cannot tell you the coffee is excellent and the seating is a punishment. Six axes can. That is the whole idea.
