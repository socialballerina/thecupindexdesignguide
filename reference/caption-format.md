# Caption format

```
📍 [Venue] @[handle]

☕ Latte: X/10

🎨 Vibe: X/10

🍽️ Food: X/10

💰 Price: X/10

📍 Location: X/10

🔁 Repeatability: X/10

+ merit
− demerit

[Two or three dry sentences.]

#thecupindex #hongkongcoffee #hkcafes #coffeerating #cafe
```

## Worked example

📍 Marouf Coffee @maroufcoffee.hk

☕ Latte: 8/10

🎨 Vibe: 6/10

🍽️ Food: 8/10

💰 Price: 7/10

📍 Location: 8/10

🔁 Repeatability: 8/10

+ elaborate menu with middle eastern coffee options
+ pistachio coffee everyone raves about, deservedly
+ accessibility is great, 1min from MTR
− awkwardly placed standing tables, not cozy
− zero laptopability, more of a takeaway shop

Latte is genuinely strong and the food punches above a takeaway counter. Seating is an afterthought so plans stay short. Come for a pistachio coffee and a walk, not a work session.

#thecupindex #hongkongcoffee #hkcafes #coffeerating #cafe

## Notes

- One score per line, blank line between each. Instagram collapses single newlines.
- Merits and demerits are lowercase fragments, specific, never generic.
- Commentary says what a number cannot. No exclamation marks, no em dashes.
