01 · The brand
02
Human warmth, measured with instruments.
The Cup Index reviews cafés without a face, a personality, or a favourite barista. Every venue is scored on the same six axes and plotted on the same hexagon, so two cafés three continents apart can be compared at a glance. The brand should feel like a measuring instrument that happens to like coffee, precise first, warm second.
We are
Systematic · comparative · calm · legible · quietly opinionated through data.
We are not
Cosy · nostalgic · influencer-led · hand-lettered · never "third-wave" as a personality.
The six axes
01Latte
Chestnut quality, milk texture, temperature, consistency across two visits.
02Vibe
Light, sound level, seating density, how long you can stay before it turns.
03Food
Kitchen range and execution, not ambition. A perfect toastie scores.
04Price
Value per visit, never cheapness. Expensive and worth it scores high.
05Location
Access, walkability, and whether the street outside earns a window seat.
06Repeat
Would we come back inside 14 days? The axis that overrules charm.
02 · Logo anatomy
03
The radar is a letter.
The mark is a flat-top hexagonal radar grid: six axes, one half-scale ring, and a plotted six-point data shape with the Latte vertex called out as a solid dot. In the wordmark it substitutes for the X of INDEX, the three axis spokes read as the crossing strokes of the letter, so the mark and the type are the same object.
01
Outer hexagon = the 10-point ceiling of every axis. Flat-top orientation only.
02
Inner ring at 50% scale, drawn at 34% stroke opacity, the reference grid.
03
Three full-width spokes through the centre define the six axes and the X-form.
04
Data shape in deep honey at 18% fill. The plotted polygon is fixed in the logo, it is never re-plotted per café.
05
Solid vertex dot at the Latte axis, the only filled element in the mark.
THE sits on the cap line of Cup · INDEX is set at 78% of Cup · glyph width 1.06 × cap height
03 · Lockups
04
Primary · stacked. Default in every square and centred context.
Horizontal · for headers, footers, and wide sign-offs. THE is dropped.
Cup Index
Wordmark only · running text, legal lines, and sizes under 20px cap height.
Mark only · avatars, favicons, stamps, and any circular crop.
Rules
Only one lockup per surface. Never pair the mark with a stacked lockup.
Never re-letterspace, re-weight, or re-set the wordmark by hand.
The mark may appear alone; the wordmark may appear alone. Nothing else may join them.
The glyph substitution is not optional in the stacked and horizontal lockups.
04 · Clear space, scale & misuse
05

×

×

×

×
